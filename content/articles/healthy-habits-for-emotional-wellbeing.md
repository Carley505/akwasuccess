## Small habits compound over time

Long-term wellbeing rarely comes from dramatic overnight changes. Instead, it grows out of small daily choices that you can keep up even during busy weeks:

1. **Protect a regular sleep window:** Going to bed and waking up at consistent times helps regulate mood, memory, and energy.
2. **Move your body every day:** Even a 25-minute brisk walk outdoors lowers tension and supports heart health.
3. **Take quiet breaks from screens:** Constant notifications and financial news keep the nervous system on high alert.
4. **Stay connected:** Talking honestly with trusted family, friends, or a counsellor lightens emotional load.

> **Important note:** This article is for education and general awareness only. If you experience persistent low mood, anxiety, or physical symptoms, please consult a qualified medical or mental-health professional.
