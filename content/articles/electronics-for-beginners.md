## Four building blocks of electronics

When you open a power supply, sensor transmitter, or controller, most of the board is built from four everyday components:

### 1. Resistors
Resistors control how much current flows through a branch of a circuit and help set operating voltages for other parts.

### 2. Capacitors
Capacitors store energy in an electric field. In power supplies, they smooth out ripples after AC is converted to DC.

### 3. Diodes (and LEDs)
A diode acts like a one-way valve for electric current. Four diodes arranged in a bridge rectifier turn alternating current (AC) into direct current (DC). Light-emitting diodes (LEDs) also glow when current flows in the forward direction.

### 4. Transistors
Transistors use a small control signal to switch or amplify a much larger current—forming the basis of logic gates, PLC outputs, and modern processors.
