## Why stablecoins were created

Cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin move up and down in price every day. That volatility makes it hard to price everyday goods or hold short-term balances without taking market risk.

A **stablecoin** aims to combine blockchain transfers with a steady value—most commonly pegged 1-to-1 with the US dollar.

## How stablecoins keep their value

The most common model is **fiat-backed reserves**: an issuing company holds cash and short-term government treasury bills equal to the number of tokens in circulation, allowing verified holders to mint or redeem tokens.

## Risks to keep in mind

Even though the price aims to stay steady in USD terms, stablecoins are not risk-free:

- Exchange rates between your local currency and USD still move up and down.
- Sending tokens on the wrong blockchain network can result in lost funds.
- Issuers and platforms have their own operational and regulatory risks.
