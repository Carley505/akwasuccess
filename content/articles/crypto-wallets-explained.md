## What a wallet actually holds

Your digital assets always live on the blockchain itself. What your **wallet** holds are two important pieces of information:

- **Your public address:** Similar to an account number you share when someone sends you funds.
- **Your private key (and recovery phrase):** The secret key that signs and approves outgoing transactions.

## Two main types of wallets

### 1. Custodial wallets (Exchange accounts)
When you sign in to an exchange with an email and password, the platform holds the private keys on your behalf. If you forget your password, their support team can help you reset it—but if the platform freezes withdrawals, you do not directly control the funds.

### 2. Non-custodial wallets (Self-custody)
Apps and hardware wallets that give you a **12- or 24-word recovery phrase** put you in complete control. No company can freeze your wallet, and no support desk can recover your funds if you lose your recovery phrase.
