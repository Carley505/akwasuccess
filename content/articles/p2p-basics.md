## What is P2P?

**Peer-to-peer (P2P)** trading lets one person buy or sell digital assets directly with another person using standard payment methods such as a bank transfer or regulated payment app.

To protect both sides, reputable P2P platforms use an **escrow** system: when a trade starts, the seller's crypto is locked by the platform until payment is confirmed.

## Safety checklist for P2P trading

- **Check the real balance, not just notifications:** Scammers often send fake payment confirmation messages or doctored screenshots. Always log in to your bank or payment app directly to verify your actual settled balance before releasing crypto.
- **Match the account name:** Only pay or accept payment from an account registered in the verified name shown on the order.
- **Stay in the order chat:** Never move the conversation to external messaging apps where the platform cannot verify evidence if a dispute arises.
