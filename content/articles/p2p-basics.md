## What is P2P?

**Peer-to-peer (P2P)** trading lets one person buy or sell digital assets directly with another person using local payment methods such as M-Pesa or a bank transfer.

To protect both sides, reputable P2P platforms use an **escrow** system: when a trade starts, the seller's crypto is locked by the platform until payment is confirmed.

## Safety checklist for P2P in Kenya

- **Check the real balance, not just the SMS:** Scammers often send fake M-Pesa confirmation messages from ordinary phone numbers. Always open your M-Pesa app or dial to check your actual statement and balance before releasing crypto.
- **Match the account name:** Only pay or accept payment from an account registered in the verified name shown on the order.
- **Stay in the order chat:** Never move the conversation to WhatsApp or Telegram where the platform cannot verify evidence if a dispute arises.
