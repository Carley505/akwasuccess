## Your recovery phrase is the master key

When you create a self-custody wallet, it displays 12 or 24 English words in a specific order. Anyone who has those words can import your wallet on their own device and move all funds out—even without your phone or PIN.

## Five golden rules for recovery phrase safety

1. **Write it on paper:** Copy the words carefully in order and check your spelling twice.
2. **Keep it offline:** Do not take a photo, screenshot, or save the words in Google Drive, iCloud, email, or WhatsApp.
3. **Never type it into a web link:** Only enter a recovery phrase when restoring a wallet inside the official wallet app you installed yourself.
4. **Ignore fake support messages:** Real support teams on Telegram, X, or WhatsApp will **never** ask for your recovery phrase or ask you to "validate" or "sync" your wallet.
5. **Store a second copy safely:** Keep a backup paper copy in a separate secure physical location in case of fire or water damage.
