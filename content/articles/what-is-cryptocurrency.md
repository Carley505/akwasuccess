## Digital money you already know vs. cryptocurrency

Most of us already use digital money every day. When you send USD or local currency through a banking app or digital payment service, your bank or payment provider updates its private database to move value from your account to someone else.

**Cryptocurrency** is also digital money, but with one big difference: there is no single company in the middle keeping the ledger. Instead, thousands of computers around the world keep the same shared record together. That shared record is called a **blockchain**.

## Why people use cryptocurrency

People use cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin and stablecoins for several practical reasons:

- **Direct transfers:** You can send value directly to another person without waiting for banking hours.
- **Global reach:** Moving value across borders uses the same network as sending it across town.
- **Self-custody:** You can hold your own digital assets in a personal wallet, provided you protect your recovery phrase.

## What cryptocurrency is NOT

Cryptocurrency is **not** a shortcut to quick wealth. Prices of digital assets can rise and fall sharply in a single day. Anyone promising "guaranteed daily returns" or asking you to send money to a private group is running a scam.

> **Tip:** Before you buy or hold any digital asset, learn how wallets and recovery phrases work. Good habits protect you far better than chasing price moves.
