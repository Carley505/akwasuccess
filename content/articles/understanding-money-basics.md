## What makes money useful?

Throughout history, communities used cattle, salt, shells, precious metals, paper notes, and now digital balances as money. Whatever form it takes, sound money performs three jobs:

1. **Medium of exchange:** Everyone accepts it for goods and services, avoiding the need for barter.
2. **Unit of account:** Prices and debts can be measured in a common unit (such as KES).
3. **Store of value:** Value earned today can be saved for future needs.

## Three practical habits for everyday financial literacy

- **Know your real monthly numbers:** Write down what comes in and every recurring expense—including small mobile-money charges that add up over a month.
- **Build a safety buffer first:** Before exploring any investment, set aside an emergency fund for unexpected medical, family, or work expenses.
- **Understand before you commit:** Never put money into an asset or business you cannot explain in plain words.
